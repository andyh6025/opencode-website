Market Terms for Beginners

Ticker
the short code a stock trades under
Sector
the industry a company belongs to
Weekly change
how much the price moved since last week, in percent
Diversification
spreading picks across sectors so one bad week hurts less

All companies and prices are fictional and for learning only.
